Engineering distinction

A requested application frame rate is not the same as the physical panel refresh rate. This proposal specifically concerns a system-managed physical display mode.

03 / Technical rationale

Why give users this option?

⚡ Power & smoothness

Intermediate rates such as 40 or 50 Hz may offer useful power-versus-motion trade-offs. The actual battery benefit depends on panel driving, brightness, workload and system design, and requires measurement.

🎬 Content cadence

Additional validated rates may improve matching to 25/50 FPS or other media, depending on synchronization and playback behavior. Existing ProMotion already manages many such cases automatically.

🎛️ User preference

Some users prefer maximum fluidity; others favor longer battery life or a specific motion feel. Manual selection would be optional, while Automatic remains the default.

🛡️ System safeguards

iOS should expose only validated hardware modes and preserve thermal, accessibility, safety and power protections, with automatic fallback where necessary.

04 / Perfect Hz mathematical exploration

Exact decimal periods

For an integer frequency f, the ideal period is 1000 / f milliseconds. In seconds, 1 / f has a terminating decimal expansion exactly when the prime factors of f are only 2 and/or 5.

Important scientific qualification

05 / Requested system behavior

Proposed implementation principles

Settings → Display & Brightness → Refresh RateAutomatic by defaultManual 1 Hz selection where supportedNo third-party app required

If a panel genuinely supports every integer value from 30 to 120 Hz, that would mean 91 choices. This is an example, not a claim that current iPhones support all 91 frequencies. The operating system should only offer physically validated frequencies and preserve necessary safeguards.

The proposal requests a native setting for all users of compatible iPhones, not a public API for third-party apps.
